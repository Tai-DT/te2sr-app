Since Google mandated the 14-day closed testing requirement (12 testers continuously opted in) for personal developer accounts, and Apple tightened Guideline 4.0 (Minimum Functionality) & Guideline 5.1.1 (Privacy), release cycles that used to take 3 days now drag out to 3–4 weeks.

For software agencies, managing 15+ physical Android test devices per client and answering 20-question production questionnaires is an operational drain.

At TE2SR (Testing to Store Release), we built a specialized testing and deployment infrastructure:

✅ 12 verified testers on physical Android hardware (Zero emulators, zero policy risks)
✅ 24/7 uptime monitoring to ensure tester retention never dips below policy thresholds
✅ Production access questionnaire & bug reproduction logs (with screenshots & device metrics)
✅ Complete Apple App Store publishing support (resolving Guideline 4.0/2.1 rejections)
✅ Flexible 2-step milestone billing (50% upfront, 50% upon store approval)
